## Overview

**Who Is My Leader?** is an experimental platform that explores the relationship between an individual's political preferences and the perspectives of political leaders.

Rather than asking users to choose a party or identify with a political label, the project aims to examine their opinions across different issues and compare them with the publicly expressed positions of political leaders.

The result is an exploration of political alignment — offering a way to discover whose viewpoints may be closest to your own.

## The Idea

Political identity is rarely one-dimensional.

Two people may agree on economic policy but disagree on social issues. A person may support one leader's approach to governance while preferring another's position on foreign policy.

This project explores that complexity.

Its central question is simple:

**If your opinions were compared with those of political leaders, who would you align with most?**

## How It Works

The platform is designed around three broad stages:

1. **Explore your views** — Respond to questions covering different political, economic, and social issues.
2. **Compare perspectives** — Evaluate your responses against the documented positions or viewpoints of political leaders.
3. **Discover alignment** — Explore the leaders whose perspectives most closely correspond to your answers.

The results are intended to encourage exploration and reflection, not prescribe political choices.

## Project Principles

- **Political neutrality:** No political party or leader is promoted or endorsed.
- **Transparency:** The basis of comparisons and the limitations of the methodology should be clearly communicated.
- **Nuance:** Political alignment should not be reduced to a single label or simplistic classification.
- **Evidence:** Leader positions should be grounded in credible, attributable sources.
- **User autonomy:** Users should be free to interpret their results independently.

## What This Project Is Not

- It is not an election prediction platform.
- It does not tell users whom to vote for.
- It does not claim to determine a person's complete political identity.
- It does not treat political alignment as a measure of intelligence, morality, or personal worth.

Any alignment score or probability-like result should be understood as an estimate produced by the project's methodology, not an objective measurement of political identity.

## Research & Methodology

The quality of the results depends on how political positions are represented, how questions are designed, and how similarities are calculated.

This project aims to make those decisions understandable and open to scrutiny.

Areas of interest include:

- Political opinion representation
- Multi-dimensional similarity measurement
- Question design and response weighting
- Uncertainty and confidence estimation
- Source reliability and position attribution
- Bias, ambiguity, and limitations in political comparisons

Detailed methodology and references can be documented here as the project evolves.

## Project Status

<em>Experimental / In Development</em>

The project is an evolving exploration of political perspectives, data representation, and comparative analysis.

Features, supported regions, political leaders, and methodology may change as development progresses.

## Contributing

## Disclaimer

Who Is My Leader? is an independent experimental project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any political party, government, candidate, or political organization.

The results are informational and exploratory. They should not be interpreted as voting recommendations or definitive assessments of political beliefs.
